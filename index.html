About Orlando Perez

The Orlando Perez Show is hosted by Orlando Perez, bringing together real conversations, diverse perspectives, interviews, current events, and topics that matter. Born and raised in Los Angeles to Cuban parents, Orlando brings a unique perspective shaped by life experiences, culture, and a genuine curiosity about the world. This is a place for honest discussions, fresh ideas, and conversations without boundaries.
